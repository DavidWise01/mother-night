The People

American playwright turned Nazi radio propagandist while secretly transmitting coded US intelligence; narrates from prison.

Campbell's beloved actress wife, presumed lost on the Eastern Front.

Helga's younger sister, who later impersonates her.

Campbell's painter friend in New York — secretly a Soviet spy.
